ABOUT US

AI-Based Animal Detection & Intrusion Monitoring System

Smart protection for agricultural fields using AI-based animal detection and timely alerts.

🌾 What Our Project Does

Our project is designed to help farmers protect their agricultural fields from unexpected animal intrusion. The system uses a camera and Artificial Intelligence to identify animals entering a monitored farm area.

When an animal is detected inside the restricted field area, the system checks the intrusion and assesses the risk. If an alert is required, the farmer and agriculture officer can be notified so that they can respond safely and quickly.

🎯 Why We Built This

Farmers may lose crops when wild or stray animals enter agricultural fields. Detecting the animal early can give farmers more time to take safe preventive action and inform the responsible authorities.

Our goal is to provide an easy-to-use monitoring system that supports farmers, improves awareness of field intrusion, and helps reduce crop damage.

⚙️ How the Project Works
1. Camera MonitoringThe field is monitored using a camera or CCTV feed.
2. AI DetectionThe AI system analyses the captured image or video and detects animals.
3. Intrusion CheckThe detected animal is checked against the defined farm/restricted zone.
4. Risk AssessmentThe system determines the appropriate alert level.
5. NotificationAn alert can be sent to the farmer and agriculture officer.
6. Safe ResponseThe user can follow the safety recommendations shown by the system.
👨‍🌾 Benefits for Farmers
  • Early awareness when an animal enters the monitored field.
  • Automatic AI-based animal detection.
  • Field intrusion monitoring through restricted zones.
  • Alerts for important incidents.
  • Farmer and officer notification support.
  • Detection and incident history for later review.
  • Safety-oriented recommendations for responding to an intrusion.
🛡️ Our Safety Approach

The system is designed to support safe response. It does not encourage harming, chasing, trapping, poisoning, or attacking animals.

The purpose of the project is to detect intrusion early, provide useful information, and help farmers and responsible officers take appropriate preventive action.

🚀 Our Vision

We aim to build a practical smart-farming solution where AI-based monitoring can help protect crops, improve response time, and support safer coexistence between agriculture and wildlife.
